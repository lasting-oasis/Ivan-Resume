MiCar

Founder · 2026 · lasting-oasis.github.io/Micar-com

Your car is the second-biggest thing you own and the only one you never check. MiCar treats it like the asset it is: valued, tracked, graded from 1 to 10 for life, and kept in a garage that is a real 3D town you can drive through. Three projects live inside it.

01 · Live

Nexus Valuation

How the prices were sourced
Official sticker prices for 60 models (2016–2026), the EPA’s list of every model sold in the US, and a weekly used-car price index from the Bureau of Labor Statistics. Listing sites forbid scraping, so instead of breaking their terms, visitors can paste a listings page or pull live dealer prices with one click.
The strategy
A four-layer market model: model resale, demand traits, supply and demand, then real comps. The depreciation curve is fitted to 1,925 real asking prices across 56 models, and a regression test fails the build if any model drifts.
The product
Trade-in, private-party and dealer prices with a range and a confidence rating, a five-year forecast, printable dealer window sheets with QR codes, English and Spanish, 102 automated tests.
02 · Prototype finished

MiCar Tuner → World engine

A finished, playable prototype in one file: tire and weight-transfer physics, a turbo and nitrous, six race types, a garage with a dyno and parts. Now being refined and ported in as MiCar World’s driving engine, with one driving model everywhere and each car’s specs drawn from real trim and EPA data, so a 2019 Civic drives like a 2019 Civic.

03 · In development

MiCar World

A multiplayer 3D dealership trainer: buy at auction, recondition, finance and sell, and learn how a real lot makes money without paying for your mistakes. Includes a shop back office, and car models generated in Blender by script.
